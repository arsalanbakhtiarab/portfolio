02 / Undergraduate research

Trade Genie
& financial sentiment.

An academic investigation into financial-news sentiment and forecasting, developed as part of the undergraduate final-year project.

Read the study ↓Contact →

Research question

Can language signals add useful context to a market-facing forecast?

The research focused on financial-news sentiment classification with fine-tuned BERT and GPT-2.

Trade Genie used this research within a real-time stock-forecasting concept, combining an LSTM forecasting component with sentiment scoring in a Streamlit dashboard.

Study structure

Keep the research question, method, and claim separate.

01

Language

Financial-news inputs were prepared for sentiment classification through a fine-tuning workflow.

02

Forecasting

An LSTM-based forecasting component formed part of the wider academic product concept.

03

Interface

A Streamlit dashboard made the research outputs easier to explore as a unified concept.

Academic framing

Research is most useful when its limitations are visible.

What this project represents

Trade Genie is presented as academic work: an opportunity to connect NLP, time-series forecasting, and product thinking. It should not be interpreted as a commercial trading system or an investment recommendation.

  • Research topic: stock sentiment analysis using fine-tuned BERT and GPT-2.
  • Product concept: a Streamlit view that combined forecasting and sentiment context.
  • Academic emphasis: separate the experimental finding from a production claim.

Methods

Tools explored

Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, BERT, GPT-2, LSTM forecasting, and Streamlit.
