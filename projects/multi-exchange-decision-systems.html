Context

Trading infrastructure is a systems problem before it is a model problem.

The platform connects multi-exchange market data, time-series storage, feature and regime processing, strategy research, backtesting, live execution, and a monitoring interface.

This record reflects engineering responsibilities evidenced in day-to-day work. It does not claim ownership of every platform component or present unsupported performance metrics.

Engineering decisions

The difficult work was often at the edges.

Reliability across moving parts

Representative debugging included timezone and resampling issues, look-ahead bias in multi-timeframe signals, strategy/ledger drift, exchange execution mismatches, and migration of PostgreSQL infrastructure from Windows to an Ubuntu VPS.

  • Worked with Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kraken Futures, Hyperliquid, and MetaTrader 5 integrations.
  • Used Python, PostgreSQL / TimescaleDB, Optuna, MLflow, Git, cron automation, and React-facing monitoring surfaces.
  • Focused on technical traceability: what happened, where it happened, and what must be reconciled next.
